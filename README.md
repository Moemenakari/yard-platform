# YARD Platform

Platform for **YARD (Youth Advocating for Rights & Development)** — a
youth-led initiative of SHiFT Social Innovation Hub in Tripoli, Lebanon.

🔗 **Live:** https://yardplatform.org

## About

YARD trains young people to lead campaigns, run research, deliver training
and coordinate emergency response across North Lebanon. The platform
presents that work to partners and funders, and gives young people a clear
route into the programmes.

## Pages

| Route | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Campaigns, trainings, impact timeline and humanitarian response |
| `/who-we-are` | The organisation, its model and its team |
| `/success-stories` | Participant stories and outcomes |
| `/partner-with-us` | Partnership and funding enquiries |

## What I worked on

Delivered with one partner, from requirements to launch:

- Ran requirement sessions with the organisation and turned them into an
  information architecture and page structure
- Built a shared design system — typography, colour tokens, spacing and
  reusable components — applied across every page
- Responsive layout across mobile, tablet and desktop
- Structured the content for each programme area with the team
- SEO foundation: metadata, generated sitemap, `robots.txt`
- Domain setup, deployment and hand-off
